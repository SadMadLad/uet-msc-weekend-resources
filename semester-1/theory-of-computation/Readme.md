# Theory of Computation

## Books

- ["Introduction to Computer Theory" by Daniel I. A. Cohen](<./books/Introduction To Computer Theory.pdf>)

## Preparation Tips

- Attending lectures regularly, taking notes helped me the most. Check out my notes under `lecture-notes/` folder. They may help you.
- Some questions during quizzes, mids and finals can appear from Cohen's book, from end of the chapter exercises. The solution manual and the book is present under `books/` directory.
  - *Note: Finding solutions to exact questions can get tedious from solutions manual because of version mismatch. However, majority of questions do have the solutions in the solution manual. If you find a better manual, feel free to create a PR*.
